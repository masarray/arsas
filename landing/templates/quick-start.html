1

Verify the Windows package

Download the installer or portable ZIP, compare SHA-256, and review the unsigned Authenticode status. SmartScreen may show an unrecognized publisher warning.

Verify package
2

Confirm the approved network boundary

Use only the authorized laboratory, FAT, SAT or commissioning network. TCP port 102 is required for ordinary MMS. Npcap is required for raw-Ethernet GOOSE and SMV capture.

Port 102 diagnostics
3

Choose the workspace

Select Engineering to add or inspect an IED, or IO List FAT to import the legacy sheet, Rev.3 workbook or portable project. Once FAT is loaded, switching back and forth uses hide/show without another file dialog.

4

Prove the intended signal identity

In Engineering, inspect IED, reference, value, quality, timestamp and acquisition source together. In Rev.3 FAT, preserve the exact Event Log Search Reference and keep the readable Report Display Reference separate.

5

Run the bounded workflow

For IO FAT, obtain a good OFF baseline and capture a new OFF → ON → OFF sequence. For live Engineering, begin with a known read-only point and identify whether its acquisition source is reporting or polling before expanding to files or guarded control.

6

Enter GOOSE or SMV from the IED card

GOOSE resolves the routed capture adapter and starts receive-only capture when unique. SMV opens the selected IED context and captures a bounded two-cycle raw waveform. Manual adapter selection remains the safe fallback.

7

Stop, review and preserve evidence

Retain exact diagnostics. Stop and seal the FAT session before exporting Excel, executive PDF or .arsas. Do not perform active control or configuration work without explicit procedure and authority.

Expected outcome

Attributable evidence—not universal device approval.
