Can ARSAS use an approved IO List for FAT?
Yes on the current mainline for imported SDI points. ARSAS validates the workbook, binds points to one live IED, requires OFF → ON → OFF evidence, and exports Excel, native PDF or a portable .arsas project. Check the stable release scope before download.
Can ARSAS generate SCL directly from an IED IP address?
Yes. After complete live discovery, ARSAS can generate an Edition 2 IID or Edition 1 ICD, plus companion evidence. A selected live RCB can also become a bounded Edition 1 or Edition 2 CID baseline.
Does ARSAS claim support for every IEC 61850 vendor?
No. Compatibility evidence remains service-specific and every receiving system must be validated.
Can ARSAS perform active control?
Guarded Direct and SBO workflows exist, but active operation requires an approved procedure, plant authority and independent safety checks.
