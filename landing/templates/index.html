IP-to-SCL integration workflow

Generate a cleaner SCL baseline from live relay evidence.

Complete MMS discovery can generate an Edition 2 IID or Edition 1 ICD directly from the connected IED. For a bounded legacy or gateway workflow, audit the live RCBs, choose one intended reporting path and export an Edition 1 or Edition 2 CID baseline with verified DataSet evidence.

Real application evidence

See the working product—not a marketing mockup.

Current Windows screenshots show independent IED sessions, unified live values, event correlation, GOOSE supervision and diagnostics.

Can ARSAS generate SCL directly from an IED IP address?
Yes. After a successful complete live discovery, ARSAS can generate an Edition 2 IID or Edition 1 ICD, plus companion evidence. A selected live RCB can also become a bounded Edition 1 or Edition 2 CID baseline.
Does ARSAS claim support for every IEC 61850 vendor?
No. The export is vendor-neutral and schema-aware, but compatibility evidence remains service-specific and every receiving system must be validated.
Why can SmartScreen show an unknown publisher?
The current public Windows binaries are not Authenticode-signed. Verify the published SHA-256 before use.
Can ARSAS perform active control?
Guarded Direct and SBO workflows exist, but active operation requires an approved procedure, plant authority and independent safety checks.
