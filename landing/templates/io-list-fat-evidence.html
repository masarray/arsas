Controlled FAT sequence

From workbook identity to ordered process evidence.

Automatic transition testing is deliberately focused on approved SDI digital indications. Unsupported, command, duplicate and incomplete-reference rows remain visible instead of being silently converted into another test type.

01

Import the approved source

ARSAS accepts the legacy ARSAS_SIGNAL_IMPORT contract and the Rev.3 FAT_Points_Import plus Document_Control contract. Project identity, IED endpoint, TestPointId, data type, source state text, exact IEC reference, data attribute, functional constraint and review reason remain explicit.

02

Review readiness without invented telegrams

Missing IED, IP, FC, data attribute or IEC reference stays blocked. Duplicate keys and active-command points stay in review scope. The exact Event Log Search Reference is preserved for log correlation, while the Report Display Reference is used only for readable customer traceability.

03

Connect and bind one IED

ARSAS discovers the live MMS model, normalizes known display wrappers without fuzzy name guessing, and accepts only one unique signal match. Real connection, discovery, binding and acquisition readiness drive the determinate card progress.

04

Capture ordered state evidence

A good-quality OFF image arms the point. ARSAS then accepts a new OFF → ON transition followed by its ON → OFF return. Every accepted item retains quality, timestamp and acquisition source; startup, reconnect, duplicate, steady-state or out-of-order images never become false edge evidence.

05

Continue safely

Completed evidence rows are protected before preflight, live preparation and session start. Switching to Engineering hides the loaded FAT workspace; returning shows the same project, selected IED, evidence and progress without another file dialog.

06

Stop, seal and export

Stop the active session, verify the append-only journal, and export the Excel result, executive PDF or complete portable project. Loading another workbook or project remains an explicit replacement action.

What ARSAS rejects

No false success from startup, ambiguity or missing identity.

  • Initial ON and reconnect first image do not count as transitions
  • Bad quality and broken continuity remain review conditions
  • Ambiguous live-model matches are not guessed
  • Missing Rev.3 IEC references remain blocked
  • SAI and SDO rows are not silently treated as SDI FAT
PASS rule

Two accepted edges inside one trustworthy continuity window.

  • Good OFF baseline
  • New OFF → ON transition
  • Accepted ON evidence
  • New ON → OFF transition
  • Accepted OFF evidence after ON
