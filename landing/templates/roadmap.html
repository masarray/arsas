Remaining priority sequence

Expand evidence breadth without weakening the shipped SDI workflow.

Roadmap order follows engineering value, failure visibility and reusable engine capability rather than feature-count marketing.

01

Analog indication campaigns

Add scaling, tolerance, units and acceptance bands without treating raw values as calibrated process proof.

02

Output and feedback test plans

Connect command steps to typed control evidence and process feedback behind explicit authority gates.

03

Reusable multi-service campaigns

Add GOOSE, Sampled Values, file-transfer, time-sync and control steps with cross-service evidence correlation.

04

Engineer review and formal acceptance

Add exceptions, witness fields, approval roles and a carefully designed signature model without claiming legal effect prematurely.

05

Visual SCL authoring and production-grade SMV

Deepen validated project authoring, stream binding, scaling, quality supervision and sustained-performance evidence.

Not a promise calendar

Maturity labels are stronger than speculative dates.

Features move from research to preview and available only when failure semantics, performance and engineering boundaries are sufficiently understood.

Current users first
